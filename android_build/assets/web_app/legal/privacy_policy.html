OFFICIAL POLICY

DualMark Studio Privacy Policy

Package ID: com.dualmark.studio | Effective Date: October 6, 2026

DualMark Studio is designed from the ground up as an air-gapped, zero-telemetry, offline-first industrial packaging and supply chain workstation.

1. Zero Remote Data Collection

DualMark Studio does not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any user data, packaging specifications, barcode numbers, product identifiers, GTINs, serial numbers, lot codes, or customer records. All data entered into DualMark Studio remains solely on your local device.

2. Ephemeral Hardware Camera Usage

DualMark Studio requests access to the device camera (android.permission.CAMERA) solely for real-time optical barcode scanning and document perspective dewarping. Camera video frames are processed ephemerally in device RAM and are never recorded, stored on persistent disk, or transmitted to any remote server.

A manual photo file picker fallback is always available for environments where physical camera access is restricted or prohibited.

3. Location & GPS Data

Geographic coordinates (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) are accessed only on user demand when explicitly clicking "Acquire GPS" within the FDA FSMA Rule 204 Critical Tracking Event (CTE) logger. This geotag is embedded locally into your exported PDF compliance dossier and is never transmitted off-device.

4. Third-Party Libraries & Telemetry

DualMark Studio contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero user tracking scripts, and zero analytical trackers. Network activity is limited exclusively to optional on-demand queries to the official GS1 GEPIR lookup endpoint when initiated by the user.

5. Contact & Data Inquiries
